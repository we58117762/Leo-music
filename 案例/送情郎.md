## 音乐构思

**核心画面**：嘴上笑着送人走，唱到后来才发现舍不得。

方案 A：北方传统小调与当代民谣交替。几乎清唱的开头有俏皮韵味，现代段落木吉他落地，东门／南门小调有轻巧三弦、木管和儿化，间奏让情绪呼吸；后半段回归克制、越来越不舍。尾句撤去乐器，留真实的人声。这里的趣味不靠夸张搞笑。**本曲歌词按 Leo 特别要求采用拼音，而不是全部歌曲的通用规则。**

### Style of Music（此前试听表现较好的版本）

~~~text
Mandarin acoustic folk with northern Chinese xiaodiao, a farewell that starts with a smile and ends in quiet longing. One young male lead: warm, clear, chest-grounded, natural Mandarin. Open almost a cappella with a playful folk tune, subtle northern erhua, gentle pitch scoops and relaxed spoken-sung rhythm; never comedic. Acoustic guitar enters for intimate modern folk verses, sung sincerely with light natural vibrato. East and South gate refrains brighten with plucked sanxian, lilting hand percussion, woodwind replies and graceful ornamentation. An instrumental break breathes with tender strings. In Verse 2, pull back to exposed guitar as farewell sinks in. West and North gate refrains keep their folk lilt and gentle erhua but gradually grow wistful, the smile fading. On the last repeated line, instruments fall away, leaving a lingering, nearly sighing solo voice. Preserve original melody and lyric pacing. No EDM, rock climax or exaggerated opera.
~~~

### Exclude

comic spoken interjections, exaggerated theatrical caricature
